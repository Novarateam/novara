# Novara Constitution

This Constitution defines the principles that guide every decision made within Novara.

These principles apply to humans, AI agents, software, and future businesses built by Novara.

---

## Principle 1 — Automation Over Repetition

If a task is performed repeatedly, it should be evaluated for automation.

Humans should spend their time on strategy, creativity, and decision-making—not repetitive execution.

---

## Principle 2 — Systems Over Shortcuts

We build systems that last.

Quick fixes are acceptable only when they do not create long-term technical debt.

---

## Principle 3 — Quality Before Scale

Scaling poor-quality systems creates bigger problems.

Quality must be established before automation is increased.

---

## Principle 4 — Providers Are Replaceable

Novara depends on capabilities, not vendors.

Any external provider should be replaceable without changing the overall architecture.

---

## Principle 5 — Every Decision Is Measurable

Every important action should produce data.

If we cannot measure performance, we cannot improve it.

---

## Principle 6 — Learn Continuously

Every success and every failure should improve the system.

Learning is a permanent process, not a feature.

---

## Principle 7 — Security Before Convenience

Access is granted only where necessary.

Every autonomous action should respect defined permissions and security boundaries.

---

## Principle 8 — Human Goals, Autonomous Execution

Humans define objectives.

Novara determines the most effective way to achieve them within approved boundaries.

---

## Principle 9 — Transparency

Important decisions made by AI must be explainable and traceable.

Logs, reasoning, and outcomes should be available for review when appropriate.

---

## Principle 10 — Build for Decades

Every architectural decision should assume Novara will continue evolving for many years.

We optimise for adaptability rather than short-term convenience.
