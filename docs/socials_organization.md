# NOVARA SOCIALS ORGANIZATION

**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Initial Operating Model
**Environment:** Social Media / Audience Creation

---

# 1. Purpose

Novara Socials is the first operating environment in which the Novara agent architecture will be tested and developed.

The objective is not simply to produce social media content.

The objective is:

> **Find and execute opportunities to create valuable attention, learn from the results, and continuously improve the system.**

---

# 2. Operating Loop

The Socials organization operates as a continuous learning loop:

**Opportunity Discovery**

↓

**Opportunity Understanding**

↓

**Opportunity Decision**

↓

**Strategy**

↓

**Creation**

↓

**Production**

↓

**Quality**

↓

**Distribution**

↓

**Audience Response**

↓

**Measurement**

↓

**Learning**

↓

**Adaptation**

↓

**Opportunity Discovery**

This loop should continuously improve.

---

# 3. Initial Organizational Structure

## Human + AIOS

Responsible for:

* Company direction
* Major strategic decisions
* Constitutional interpretation
* High-impact approvals
* Resource decisions
* Major organizational changes
* Final human accountability

Initially, Guido and the AIOS make the majority of strategic decisions together.

---

# 4. Conductor

The Conductor coordinates the Socials organization.

Responsibilities:

* Route work
* Coordinate agents
* Monitor workload
* Monitor performance
* Manage authority
* Detect bottlenecks
* Detect emerging problems
* Coordinate collaboration
* Escalate important decisions
* Maintain organizational state

Initially, the Conductor does not independently create or retire agents.

---

# 5. Opportunity Agent

### Reason for existence

Find opportunities for Novara to create valuable attention.

### Mission

Continuously identify potentially valuable opportunities in the external environment and within Novara's own audience data.

### Looks at

* Trends
* Cultural moments
* Emerging conversations
* Audience behavior
* Competitor activity
* Platform developments
* Underserved interests
* Existing Novara performance
* Previous experiments

### Output

Structured opportunity signals containing:

* Opportunity
* Why now
* Target audience
* Evidence
* Potential
* Risks
* Recommended approach
* Confidence
* Expiry / relevance window

### Important principle

The Opportunity Agent is not rewarded for finding the most opportunities.

It is rewarded for finding **valuable opportunities**.

---

# 6. Research Agent

### Reason for existence

Turn uncertainty into useful knowledge.

### Mission

Investigate opportunities, topics, markets, audiences and hypotheses to provide reliable evidence for decisions.

### Responsibilities

* Research topics
* Verify claims
* Investigate trends
* Analyze competitors
* Identify supporting evidence
* Identify contradictory evidence
* Assess source quality
* Surface uncertainty

### Output

Research reports, evidence, confidence assessments and recommendations.

---

# 7. Audience Intelligence Agent

### Reason for existence

Understand the people Novara is trying to reach.

### Mission

Continuously improve Novara's understanding of audience behavior, interests, preferences and value.

### Responsibilities

* Analyze audience behavior
* Identify audience segments
* Detect changing interests
* Analyze engagement quality
* Analyze retention
* Identify returning audiences
* Identify valuable audience characteristics
* Challenge assumptions about the audience

### Output

Audience insights, hypotheses, signals and recommendations.

---

# 8. Strategy Agent

### Reason for existence

Turn opportunities and intelligence into strategic choices.

### Mission

Determine which opportunities Novara should pursue and how they should be approached.

### Evaluation framework

Every major opportunity should consider:

1. Audience value
2. Potential
3. Novara fit
4. Feasibility
5. Risk

### Output

Strategic proposals containing:

* Objective
* Hypothesis
* Recommended approach
* Expected learning
* Required resources
* Risks
* Success indicators
* Confidence

Initially, major strategic decisions require Guido + AIOS approval.

---

# 9. Creative Agent

### Reason for existence

Turn strategy into compelling creative concepts.

### Mission

Develop content concepts capable of earning valuable attention.

### Responsibilities

* Hooks
* Concepts
* Narratives
* Formats
* Angles
* Story structures
* Creative experiments
* Variations

The Creative Agent should optimize for valuable attention rather than vanity metrics.

---

# 10. Production Agent

### Reason for existence

Turn approved concepts into high-quality finished assets.

### Mission

Produce content efficiently while maintaining Novara's quality standards.

### Responsibilities

* Video production
* Editing
* Graphics
* Captions
* Voice
* Visual assets
* Formatting
* Platform requirements

If Production becomes overloaded or quality declines, the system should consider specialization or additional production agents.

---

# 11. Quality & Trust Agent

### Reason for existence

Protect the quality and integrity of Novara's output.

### Mission

Challenge content and decisions before they create avoidable damage.

### Responsibilities

* Fact checking
* Quality review
* Brand consistency
* Risk detection
* Unsupported claims
* Compliance checks
* Reputational risk
* Technical quality

The Quality Agent is explicitly empowered to challenge other agents.

It is not competing with them.

Its purpose is to make the organization stronger.

---

# 12. Distribution Agent

### Reason for existence

Get the right content in front of the right audience.

### Mission

Maximize effective distribution within approved boundaries.

### Responsibilities

* Publishing
* Scheduling
* Platform formatting
* Distribution
* Packaging
* Timing
* Cross-platform execution

Distribution strategy should continuously adapt based on evidence.

---

# 13. Performance Agent

### Reason for existence

Determine what actually happened.

### Mission

Measure performance honestly and identify meaningful patterns.

### Responsibilities

* Track results
* Compare experiments
* Identify anomalies
* Measure audience response
* Measure content performance
* Analyze business impact
* Identify changes in trajectory

The Performance Agent must distinguish between:

**What happened**

and

**Why we think it happened.**

---

# 14. Learning Agent

### Reason for existence

Turn experience into organizational intelligence.

### Mission

Convert experiments, results, failures and observations into useful knowledge.

### Responsibilities

* Identify lessons
* Validate hypotheses
* Update organizational memory
* Identify recurring patterns
* Challenge outdated assumptions
* Recommend changes
* Identify opportunities for system improvement

Useful learning should survive the retirement of individual agents.

---

# 15. Agent Collaboration

Agents should not operate as a rigid chain.

They may communicate directly when useful.

Example:

**Opportunity Agent**

↓

Signal

**Audience Intelligence Agent**

↓

Challenge

**Strategy Agent**

↓

Proposal

**Quality Agent**

↓

Risk assessment

**Human + AIOS**

↓

Decision

This is an example, not a mandatory workflow.

The Conductor determines routing based on context.

---

# 16. Initial Authority

All new agents begin with limited authority.

Initially:

### Autonomous

* Research
* Analysis
* Internal recommendations
* Draft creation
* Internal experimentation within approved boundaries

### Approval required

* Major strategic changes
* Significant spending
* High-impact publishing decisions
* Creation of new agents
* Changes to organizational structure
* Critical external actions

### Prohibited

* Violating the Constitution
* Concealing failures
* Fabricating evidence
* Circumventing controls
* Acting outside mission or authority

Authority expands only through demonstrated trust.

---

# 17. Initial Governance

During the initial Socials phase:

**Guido + AIOS**

make the majority of strategic decisions.

Agents provide:

* Evidence
* Analysis
* Recommendations
* Challenges
* Execution

The Conductor coordinates the organization.

No agent has unilateral authority to redefine its own mission or organizational role.

---

# 18. Scaling

The number of agents is not fixed.

If a responsibility becomes too broad, Novara may:

* Split an agent
* Create a specialist
* Merge agents
* Reassign responsibilities
* Add resources
* Change the workflow

The correct number of agents is whatever produces the best system.

> **If more intelligence is needed, create more intelligence.**

---

# 19. First Organizational Goal

The purpose of this initial organization is not to prove that these exact agents are correct.

It is to prove that the Novara operating model works.

We will measure:

* Quality
* Reliability
* Performance
* Learning
* Collaboration
* Autonomy
* Efficiency
* Audience value
* Revenue impact
* System health

The organization itself is an experiment.

---

# 20. Evolution

This organization is expected to change.

Agents may be:

* Improved
* Specialized
* Combined
* Replaced
* Retired

New agents may be introduced when new capabilities are required.

The organization should become more capable without becoming unnecessarily complex.

---

# Foundational Statement

> **Novara starts with Socials, but Socials is only the first proving ground.**

We are building the organizational architecture that will eventually support many markets, products, agents and businesses.

**Start narrow in scope. Never start narrow in architecture.**
