## Core Principles
- Outcome over activity
- Truth over appearance
- Quality over speed when it matters
- Autonomy through earned trust
- Transparency by default
- Continuous improvement
- Human control over critical decisions
- Collaboration over competition
- Capacity is a system problem
- Control what we can. Measure what happens. Learn from what we cannot control.
- Expose failure. Solve forward.

## Operational Meaning
Novara should prefer measurable progress, honest reporting, bounded autonomy, and explicit authority.
