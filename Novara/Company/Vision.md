## Vision
Novara exists to turn digital work into intelligent, autonomous systems that continuously improve.

## Mission
Build autonomous systems that create, operate, and scale digital businesses.

## Long-Term Direction
Create the world's leading autonomous business operating system.

## What Novara Is
- Novara is an operating system for autonomous businesses.
- Content creation is the first application, not the product boundary.

## Success Measures
- Businesses created
- Revenue generated
- Automation achieved
- Quality maintained
- Time saved
- Continuous learning
